Harikumar leads the laboratory's work on the role of inflammation in physiology, the innate immune response, and in pathophysiology, principally cancer.

He took his PhD in Biochemistry at Amala Cancer Research Center, Thrissur, in 2008, after an MSc from Nagpur University. He was a research intern at the UT MD Anderson Cancer Center and a post-doctoral associate at Virginia Commonwealth University before returning to India as a DBT Ramalingaswami Fellow. He has been at BRIC-RGCB since, as Scientist C, Scientist E-I, E-II, and now Scientist F.

He is a member of the Indian Association for Cancer Research, the American
Association of Indian Scientists in Cancer Research, and the European Federation
of Biotechnology.
