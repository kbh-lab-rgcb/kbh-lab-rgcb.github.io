# Other publications from the principal investigator

**Same format as [`../years/`](../years/) — one file per year, one paper per
paragraph.** Everything in that README applies here too: DOIs, PMIDs, bolded
author names, citations that wrap over several lines.

## What belongs in here

Papers the PI is an author on that are **not this laboratory's own work**:

- his doctoral and post-doctoral publications, from before the laboratory
  existed;
- studies he has guided, examined or co-authored in **someone else's** group.

## What belongs in `../years/` instead

Papers with **a member of this laboratory among the authors** — current people
or alumni. Those are the laboratory's own output.

## Why the two folders

The number on the home page and the count at the top of the Publications page
are the papers in `../years/` only. Keeping the two apart is what lets that
number mean "what this laboratory has published" rather than "every paper the
PI has ever been listed on".

## Moving a paper between them

Cut the paragraph out of one file and paste it into the file of the same year
in the other folder. That is the whole operation — nothing else needs changing,
and the counts, the headings and the home page all follow on the next build.

If a year file ends up empty, delete it.
