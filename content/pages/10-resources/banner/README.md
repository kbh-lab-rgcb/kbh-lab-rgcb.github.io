# Banner images

**Drop an image in this folder and it becomes the banner across the top of this
page.** Any name, any size. No other step.

- **One image** → a still banner.
- **Two or more** → they turn into a slideshow automatically, with arrows and
  dots. Visitors can also swipe on a phone.
- **None** → a plain coloured banner. The page still works.

Order is alphabetical, so name them `01-…`, `02-…` to control the sequence.

Accepted: `.jpg` `.jpeg` `.png` `.webp` `.svg` `.gif`

Big photos straight from a phone or camera are fine — they are resized
automatically when the site is built. Landscape images work best; something
around 1600 × 900 or wider is ideal.

The `.svg` files here now are placeholders. Delete them once you have real
photographs.
